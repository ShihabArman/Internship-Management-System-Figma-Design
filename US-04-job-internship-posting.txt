User Story:
  As a company, I want to post jobs and internships so that students can apply.

Screen Definition:
  - Job title field
  - Description field
  - Eligibility / qualifications field
  - Application deadline field
  - Location field
  - Submit / Post button

Acceptance Criteria:
  - All mandatory details must be filled before posting.
  - Post is visible to students only after admin approval.
  - Confirmation message is displayed after successful submission.
  - Company can edit or remove their post.
