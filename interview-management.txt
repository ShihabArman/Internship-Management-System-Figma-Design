  As an HR member, I want to manage interviews so that the selection process is organized.

  - Schedule interview form (date, time, mode)
  - Candidate notification option
  - Interview result input field
  - Interview list view

Acceptance Criteria:
  - Interview schedule is saved and linked to the correct applicant.
  - Students are notified of their scheduled interview.
  - Interview results are updated correctly after the interview.
  - HR can reschedule or cancel an interview if needed.
